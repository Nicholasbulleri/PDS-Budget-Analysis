## Key Points to Include

1. **Direct Confirmation**: Clearly state that users will be authorized
2. **Proactive Solutions**: Offer specific ways to ensure compliance
3. **Collaboration**: Show willingness to work with them on requirements
4. **Transparency**: Offer to provide user lists or implement audit trails
5. **Professional Tone**: Keep it collaborative, not defensive

## What to Avoid

- Don't be vague about authorization
- Don't make it sound like you're trying to bypass requirements
- Don't assume they'll just grant access without verification
- Don't be defensive about the requirement
